What still needs review

Growth plate & tibial geometry

Resolve the medial interface, maximum-height section, and first articular / growth-plate appearances. The 2.911 mm tilted junction chord is not the paper’s tibial width.

Bone regions & outgrowths

Define medial/lateral subchondral and trabecular ROIs before BV/TV or trabecular metrics. Review osteophyte boundaries and separate joint mineralization before quantifying them.

Inclusion decisions

Review tibial-margin fragments, sesamoids, and the proximal patellar ossification. Mineralized label volume is not whole-organ volume. Cartilage thickness and mineral density are not established by these data.

Evidence & provenance
